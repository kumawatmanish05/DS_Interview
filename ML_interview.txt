## Learning Theory

1. Describe bias and variance with examples.
-- 🎯 Bias and variance are two types of errors in a machine learning model.

🔹 Bias:
Bias is the error due to wrong or overly simple assumptions in the model.
➡️ High bias → Underfitting (model misses patterns)

🔹 Variance:
Variance is the error due to the model being too sensitive to the training data.
➡️ High variance → Overfitting (model memorizes data, fails on new data)

📌 Example:
• Using a linear model for nonlinear data → High Bias ❌
• Using a very deep decision tree → High Variance ❌

🎯 Key Point:
✔️ Goal = Balance Bias ⚖️ and Variance for good generalization

⚡ One-line version:
Bias causes underfitting due to simple assumptions, while variance causes overfitting due to sensitivity to data.
